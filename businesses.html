Our Business Portfolio

Diverse Interests. Shared Vision.

Validius Group operates across a range of business sectors, bringing together industry knowledge, strategic partnerships and a commitment to sustainable growth. Each venture represents our ambition to build meaningful value and explore new opportunities.

01 / JEWELLERY

Bengal Diamond

Gold & Jewellery Manufacturing

Focused on gold and jewellery manufacturing, Bengal Diamond aims to deliver quality craftsmanship, refined designs and lasting value in the jewellery industry.

02 / GEMSTONE CERTIFICATION

SGL Lab Bangladesh

Diamond Testing & Certification

Dedicated to diamond testing, analysis and certification services, supporting transparency and confidence in the diamond and gemstone industry through internationally recognised testing standards.

03 / AGRICULTURE

Satu Agro

Agro Processing & Agricultural Products

Engaged in rice and paddy-related business activities, including rice bran crude oil and agricultural by-products, with plans to expand into refining and branded rice bran oil production.

04 / AUTOMOTIVE

ZAC

Automotive Parts Import & Distribution

Focused on importing and distributing automotive parts from Germany, South Korea, China and India, connecting international manufacturers with the Bangladesh automotive market.

05 / REAL ESTATE

Bengal Urban Homes Ltd.

Real Estate Development

Focused on residential and property development opportunities in key growth locations, including Bashundhara and Jolshiri, with a vision for quality living and future urban development.

06 / INDUSTRIAL SOLUTIONS

Lincon Traders

Industrial Sewing Machinery

Engaged in importing and distributing industrial sewing machinery, serving the garment and manufacturing industries with machinery solutions and related equipment.

07 / ELECTRIC MOBILITY

NEX DRIVE

Electric Vehicles & Charging Infrastructure

Working towards the future of electric mobility through EV solutions, charging infrastructure and related opportunities, supporting the transition towards cleaner transportation.

08 / HEALTHCARE SERVICES

Medi Bridge

Medical Tourism & Healthcare Facilitation

Connecting patients from Bangladesh with international healthcare opportunities through medical tourism, treatment coordination and access to overseas healthcare services.
