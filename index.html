01 — Who We Are

A diversified group with a long-term perspective.

Validius Group is built around the idea that meaningful businesses create value not only through products and services, but also through trusted relationships, responsible growth and long-term vision.

We are developing a diversified group of companies with interests across multiple industries, combining established businesses with emerging opportunities and future-focused ventures.

Each company within the group operates within its own area of expertise while remaining connected through the broader vision, values and strategic direction of Validius Group.

Leadership

Visionary leadership. Long-term ambition.

Validius Group is guided by an entrepreneurial vision focused on building sustainable businesses, developing strong partnerships and creating long-term value across industries.

Fahad Kamal Lincon

Founder & Chairman

As Founder & Chairman of Validius Group, Fahad Kamal Lincon leads the group's strategic direction with a focus on entrepreneurship, business development, partnerships and long-term growth.

His vision is centered on building a diversified and internationally connected business group capable of identifying opportunities, developing meaningful enterprises and creating lasting value for partners, customers and the wider community.
